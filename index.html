Who we are and how to contact us

mitkonaft games develops Chess Prodigy: Tactical 3D. For support, privacy requests, or source-code requests, email ak.onfire@mail.ru. Please include the app name and iOS version; do not send sensitive information or passwords.

Chess content and gameplay progression

The app does not require an account. Chess positions, engine analysis, saved repertoires, puzzle favorites, tutorial completion, preferences, and gameplay progression are processed or stored on your device. The app does not send these records to a developer-operated analytics server. We do not collect anonymous gameplay progression through a separate analytics service. Local progress has no app account attached; this does not mean advertising data is anonymous.

Offline chess analysis uses engines running on your device. If you choose to export or share content, the recipient or service you select receives that content under its own privacy practices. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Unity Ads and advertising data

The app integrates the Unity Ads iOS SDK for banner and optional rewarded advertisements. Unity and its advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and operating-system details, ad impressions, clicks, video completion, and technical performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service reliability. Identifier-based advertising data can be associated with a device and should not be treated as anonymous.

Personalized ads may use permitted identifiers and activity across apps or websites to select and measure advertising, subject to your permissions and applicable consent requirements. Contextual ads use information about the app or current context rather than permission to track across other companies’ apps or websites. Contextual ad delivery can still involve network, device, measurement, and security data; refusing tracking does not mean no data is processed.

Unity processes information under its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which explains its recipients, international processing, retention, and privacy choices. Advertiser websites opened from an ad have their own policies.

App Tracking Transparency and your choices

On iOS, the app requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before initializing its advertising SDK when permission has not yet been determined. Tracking across other companies’ apps and websites and use of the advertising identifier for that purpose require your authorization. If you decline or iOS restricts tracking, the app must respect that choice; contextual ads may still be displayed. ATT permission does not replace any additional consent required by local law.

You can change permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Core chess training remains available if you decline. Rewarded videos are optional; the displayed reward is granted after successful completion. Unity may also provide privacy controls through its ads or privacy policy.

Diagnostics and support

The app has no separate developer-operated crash-reporting or telemetry SDK. Apple may provide crash or diagnostic reports according to your device analytics settings. Unity Ads may process technical diagnostics and performance information for its advertising service. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond and troubleshoot.

Sharing, retention, and security

We do not sell your local chess records or upload them to our own servers. Advertising providers receive data as described above, and support messages are handled by our email provider. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. Providers may process data in other countries with different privacy protections.

Local records remain until you remove them through available app controls or delete the app; backups are managed separately through Apple. We retain support correspondence only as needed for the request, related follow-up, or legal obligations. Advertising data retention is governed by Unity’s policy. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Privacy rights and children

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us for information we hold; use Unity’s privacy channels for information Unity controls. We cannot retrieve local-only chess records from your device.

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us. Applicable age restrictions and consent requirements must be respected for advertising; parental or device restrictions can limit ATT authorization.
